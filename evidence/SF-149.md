## What was derived rather than typed

Everything below comes from the run contract, the mission portfolio, and the
Bridge project registry. Nothing is supplied by the Owner and nothing is
invented.

* **Which mission is next** — `Portfolio.next_mission`, the frozen serial rule,
  over durable mission state.
* **Mission identity** — `work_item_id` is the portfolio's `mission_ref`; the
  context manifest is the evidence layer's seven-field record, hashed by
  `context.sha256_hex`; the idempotency key is
  `routing.expected_idempotency_key(work_item_id, manifest_hash)`, the only key
  the evidence layer will bind.
* **The live admission document** — trusted-dispatch readiness bound to the
  Bridge registry digest, an Owner ratification bound to the live shift grant's
  approval reference, and the registry's own project registration. All three
  self-hashed.
* **Acceptance gate commands** — `dev-check` becomes
  `[<checkout>/dev, check]`, derived from the gate's own
  `acceptance_gate_source` (`…:dev`). A gate id that does not name a step of
  that evaluator is refused, never guessed: `stage1_adapter` records an
  undeclared gate as `not_run`, and `not_run` is a failure.

`admission_mode: live_evidence` is the part that matters. Anything weaker
admits as a fixture and `build_preflight_report` then refuses the real
transport with `REAL_TRANSPORT_REQUIRES_LIVE_AUTHORITY`.

## Three refusals that fire before anything is dispatched

* `MISSION_CHANGES_A_REPOSITORY` — a mutating mission's gates would have to be
  re-derived at the candidate commit, in the worktree the execution layer
  creates, and no layer reports that path back. Running them against the
  baseline checkout would report the mission's own change as untested.
* `CAPABILITY_NOT_ADMISSIBLE` — the evidence layer admits `development` and
  `prototype` only. `factory-bug-lab` is registered for `bug`, so DF-2 and DF-3
  cannot be admitted as written.
* `ACCEPTANCE_GATE_NOT_DERIVABLE` — see above.

## The defect the intake exposed

`ShiftPlane.outcomes` matched a portfolio mission by `idempotency_key = ref`.
A real mission's key can never be the bare reference —
`expected_idempotency_key` mandates `work_item_id:context_manifest_hash` and
the evidence layer refuses anything else. So the frozen shift plane could not
observe a single real dogfood mission: `next_mission` would have returned DF-1
forever and `missions_in_flight` would have read zero. `outcomes` now matches
both forms and keys its answer on the mission reference.
